1. History

  • Galilei (1564–1642)), observations of bodies in motion
  • Huygens (1629–1695), the watch
  • Newton (1643–1727), Principia “laws of motion” for particles
  • Euler (1707–1783), ``equations of motion" for bodies
  • D’Alembert (1717–1783), “principle of virtual work”
  • Lagrange (1736–1813), “analytical mechanics”
  • Einstein (1879-1955), “principle of relativity”
