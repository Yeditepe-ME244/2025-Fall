1. Equations of Motion

  • Kinetics of particles moving in curved planar paths require careful consideration of the normal and tangential components, which can be resolved in any one of the below coordinates:
    • rectangular \[ \sum_i \boldsymbol{F}_x = m\,\ddot{x} \, , \hspace{1em} \sum_i \boldsymbol{F}_y = m\,\ddot{y} \]
    • cylindrical \[ \sum_i \boldsymbol{F}_r = m(\ddot{r} - r \dot{\theta}^2)\, , \hspace{1em} \sum_i \boldsymbol{F}_\theta = m(r\ddot{\theta} + 2\dot{r} \dot{\theta}) \]
